# Recalllens — rubric scorecard

## Submission strategy

Compete for the Overall Award. Do not claim the COOL or Agentic Vision special paths. The strongest evidence is the OpenCV 5 geometric pipeline, the original physical-world interaction, and the privacy-bounded AWS authorization architecture.

| Official criterion | Weight | Evidence ready | Remaining action |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Technical execution | 30% | OpenCV 5, signed device build, ten Lambda tests, stabilizer checks, five evaluator tests; 641-photo synthetic stress test and 49-pair exploratory audit | Record the updated physical-device flow and a visible failure case |
| Innovation | 20% | A real-world view acts as the key to a private memory archive | Explain the concept in the first 25 seconds of the video |
| Real-world impact | 20% | Personal place-bound recall; extensions to site notes and histories | State the target user and bounded use case clearly; avoid unsupported adoption claims |
| User experience | 10% | Camera-first capture, stable match state, locked archive | Record capture, rejection, return, and unlock as one continuous flow |
| Documentation and presentation | 10% | Technical report, Devpost copy, architecture diagram, build/deploy/test instructions | Publish the ≤5-minute video and accessible code archive |
| Cloud delivery and responsible operation | 10% | Updated deployment; live registration, authorization, bad-token and expired-signature checks passed | Show the architecture and published deployment evidence; disclose initial-request latency and why no public iOS binary is available |

## Blocking submission items

1. Confirm the Devpost account has joined the competition.
2. Keep the public repository and AWS demonstration endpoint available throughout judging.
3. Record and publish the five-minute-or-shorter demonstration video.
4. Make the continuous real-device video sufficient to assess the app without a TestFlight installation, and offer the required live screen-share through Devpost contact.
5. Paste the final report and project copy into Devpost and submit before the conservative deadline.

## Claims policy

- Describe the six-trial result as a preliminary integration pilot.
- Describe the 641-photo result as archived-photo synthetic stress testing, and the 49-pair result as a selected exploratory audit. Neither is a new field study.
- Do not generalize its accuracy to other scenes or users.
- Do not describe visual correspondence as identity, liveness, or geographic proof.
- Do not claim COOL or Agentic Vision eligibility.
